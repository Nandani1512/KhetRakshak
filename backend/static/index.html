    Why it is different

    Not a district bulletin. Not a chatbot.

    Your field, not your district

    Greenness is measured inside your own boundary, pixel by pixel, with clouds masked out.

    Warns before it hits

    IMD heavy-rain and heat-wave criteria, checked against your village's own 10-year normal.

    Every claim has a source

    Page-level citations to PAU, TNAU, ICAR-CRIDA and IMD. No source → "contact your KVK".

    Money-aware

    Today's Agmarknet prices at nearby mandis, minus your own transport cost.

    Never breaks silently

    If a data source is down, the agent says so, uses a labelled cache or continues with what it has.

    FAQ

    Questions farmers and judges ask

    Where does the data come from?

    Sentinel-2 satellite (ESA Copernicus via Microsoft Planetary Computer), Open-Meteo forecast and ERA5 history, Agmarknet mandi prices on data.gov.in, and official PAU, TNAU, ICAR-CRIDA and IMD documents. Nothing is made up.

    What if it is cloudy?

    Cloudy pixels are removed. If the field is cloudy, the last clear view is used and the confidence is lowered — and the alert says so.

    What if the AI makes a mistake?

    A verifier checks every number against the tool outputs and every advice against its cited page. Wrong drafts are sent back; anything still unsupported is replaced by "contact your local KVK".

    Which crops and languages?

    Paddy, wheat and mustard in Punjab and Tamil Nadu today; Hindi, Tamil, Punjabi and English, by text or voice.
